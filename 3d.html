04 · About

Who Is IMAX3D!?

IMAX3D! is an instrumental producer and composer crafting psychedelic hip-hop landscapes that blur the line between beats and cinematic soundscapes. Producing for over a decade, the focus stays on immersive melodies, evolving arrangements, and experimental textures that transform each track into its own world.

Drawing inspiration from the atmospheric side of modern hip-hop while pushing beyond traditional beat-making, the music explores emotion, space, and movement through layered sound design and unconventional composition. From hypnotic melodies to hard-hitting drums, every release is built to create an experience rather than simply provide a backdrop.
