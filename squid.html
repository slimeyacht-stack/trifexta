Who Is PraiseSquid?

Born in the pressure of the deep trench, PraiseSquid spent years camouflaged in the underground before surfacing with a flow slick enough to make the whole tank ripple.

The name isn't a gimmick, it's a philosophy: praise for the come-up and the ones still grinding in the dark water, squid because the flow moves in eight directions at once and you'll never see the punchline coming until it's already landed.
