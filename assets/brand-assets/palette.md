# TR!FEXTA Color Palette Definitions

## Primary Palette — "Deep Ink"

| Role | Hex | RGB | Usage |
|------|-----|-----|-------|
| Background | `#0a0a0c` | (10, 10, 12) | Page backgrounds, dark surfaces |
| Surface | `#15181b` | (21, 24, 27) | Cards, panels, elevated surfaces |
| Surface 2 | `#1c2024` | (28, 32, 36) | Secondary panels, hover states |
| Text | `#ece6da` | (236, 230, 218) | Primary text on dark |
| Muted | `#9a948a` | (154, 148, 138) | Secondary text, labels |
| Violet | `#7c5cff` | (124, 92, 255) | Primary accent, links, highlights |
| Cyan | `#38bdf8` | (56, 189, 248) | Secondary accent, data, info |
| Pink | `#ff5c8a` | (255, 92, 138) | CTAs, emphasis, energy |
| Deep Violet | `#5a3fd6` | (90, 63, 214) | Gradient endpoint, shadow |
| Ink | `#0e1012` | (14, 16, 18) | Alternative dark background |

## Line & Border

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Line | `#3a3d40` | Borders, dividers |

## White/Light Mode (future)

| Role | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| Background | `#ffffff` | Light surfaces |
| Surface | `#f5f3ef` | Cards on light |
| Text | `#1a0a10` | Primary text on light |
| Accent | `#7c5cff` | Same violet carries over |

## Gradient Definitions

| Name | From | To | Use |
|------|------|----|-----|
| Primary gradient | `#7c5cff` → `#ff5c8a` | 100deg | Logo, CTAs, highlights |
| Secondary gradient | `#7c5cff` → `#38bdf8` | 100deg | Secondary accents |
| Hero gradient | `#7c5cff` → `#5a3fd6` → `#ff5c8a` | 135deg | Banners, hero sections |
